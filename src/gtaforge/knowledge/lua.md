# Lua (FiveM / RedM)

- Single-player GTA V has no Lua runtime. Lua means a FiveM/RedM server:
  `fxmanifest.lua`, client/server scripts, `Citizen.InvokeNative`.
- `gtaforge new <name> --kind lua`, `gtaforge script lua .` (luajit -bl).
- FiveM is a separate ecosystem; do not mix its toolchains with SP asi work.

# Animations

- Build an index: `gtaforge menyoo index --folder <menyooStuff>` (PedAnimList.txt).
- Search: `gtaforge anim --pattern <part>`; clips: `gtaforge anim --dict <dict> --clip <part>`.
- Play: REQUEST_ANIM_DICT -> HAS_ANIM_DICT_LOADED -> TASK_PLAY_ANIM.
- Adult dicts (street prostitution, strip club, masturbation) are in the index
  under `mini@prostitutes@*`, `mini@strip_club@*`, `amb@code_human_in_car_mp_actions@wank@*`.
  Adults only: a character recorded as under 18 is never a participant.
- GTA VI clips: unknown until the files are readable; do not reuse V names as fact.
